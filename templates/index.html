About us

Bringing mercy, food, education and hope to underserved communities.

Founder and CEO Muwanguzi Gift Omar began Holy Harvest because he could not ignore children, widows, and families going to bed hungry and without hope. Our mission is simple: harvest souls for Christ and harvest resources to restore lives.

Through feeding programs, skills training, church outreaches, and community support, lives are being transformed one family at a time. We believe Uganda’s greatest harvest is not in the soil, but in its people. Every donation plants a seed of food, faith, and a future.

“For the sake of His sorrowful Passion, have mercy on us.” Join us as we bring God’s mercy to the streets of Uganda.

Vision

To see every vulnerable child, widow, youth, and family living a life of hope, dignity, self-reliance, and joy through God’s transforming love.

Our mission is to demonstrate God’s love by providing compassionate care, access to education, clean water, food security, healthcare, and livelihood opportunities that empower vulnerable children, families, and communities to become self-reliant and thrive.

About us

Bringing mercy, food, education and hope to underserved communities.

Founded by Muwanguzi Gift Omar, Holy Harvest Charity Ministries is a faith-based, non-profit organization dedicated to restoring hope and transforming lives through food security, clean water, education, healthcare and community empowerment.

Mission & vision

To see vulnerable families living in hope, dignity and self-reliance.

Our mission is to demonstrate God’s love by providing compassionate care, education, food security, healthcare and livelihood opportunities that empower vulnerable children and families to thrive.

Why partner with us?

Your partnership provides food, water, learning and lasting hope.

  • Nutritious meals for hungry children
  • Clean drinking water and sanitation
  • Educational and vocational opportunities
  • Support for widows and vulnerable families

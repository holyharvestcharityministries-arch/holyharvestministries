About us

Bringing mercy, food, education and hope to underserved communities.

Founder and CEO Muwanguzi Gift Omar began Holy Harvest because he could not ignore children, widows, and families going to bed hungry and without hope. Our mission is simple: harvest souls for Christ and harvest resources to restore lives.

Through feeding programs, skills training, church outreaches, and community support, lives are being transformed one family at a time. We believe Uganda’s greatest harvest is not in the soil, but in its people. Every donation plants a seed of food, faith, and a future.

“For the sake of His sorrowful Passion, have mercy on us.” Join us as we bring God’s mercy to the streets of Uganda.

About us

Founder: Muwanguzi Gift Omar

Founder & CEO of Holy Harvest Charity Ministries Uganda. I started Holy Harvest because I couldn’t ignore the children, widows, and families in our communities going to bed hungry and without hope.

Our mission is simple: Harvest souls for Christ and harvest resources to restore lives.

Through feeding programs, skills training, church outreaches, and community support, we are seeing lives transformed — one family at a time.

I believe Uganda’s greatest harvest is not in the soil, but in its people. Every donation you make plants a seed of food, faith, and a future.

“For the sake of His sorrowful Passion, have mercy on us.” Join us as we bring God’s mercy to the streets of Uganda.

Holy Harvest Charity Ministries is a Christian faith-based, non-profit organization dedicated to restoring hope and transforming lives among vulnerable children, widows, youth, and underserved communities in Uganda.

Inspired by God’s love, we work to address both immediate needs and long-term community development through food security, clean water, education, healthcare, child sponsorship, and community empowerment. We believe that every individual is created in the image of God and deserves the opportunity to live with dignity, hope, and purpose.

Our approach combines compassion with sustainable development by responding to urgent needs while equipping communities to become self-reliant and resilient.

Mission
